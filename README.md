## 📖 About the Project

**BiblioTrack** is a comprehensive web-based **Smart Library Management System** designed to transform the traditional departmental library of the **Computer Science Department, University of Peshawar**, into a modern digital platform.

The system addresses critical challenges faced by students and library staff — including inefficient manual record-keeping, difficulty in tracking issued books, inconsistent fine calculation, and limited access to digital resources — by providing a unified, intelligent, and user-friendly solution.

BiblioTrack manages both **physical books** and **digital resources (PDFs)** within a single integrated platform, offering separate portals for students and administrators to ensure secure access and efficient library operations.

> **BiblioTrack bridges the gap between physical and digital library resources — all within one seamless platform.**

## 🎯 Objectives

- Replace manual record-keeping with a fully digital workflow.
- Provide equal, fair access to library resources for all students.
- Introduce AI-driven assistance to reduce staff workload.
- Enable seamless digital resource access for off-campus learning.
- Automate fine calculation and integrate locally relevant payment systems.
- Improve overall efficiency, transparency, and user satisfaction.
