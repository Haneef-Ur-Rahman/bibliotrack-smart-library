## 📖 Overview

**BiblioTrack** is a comprehensive web-based **Smart Library Management System** designed to transform the traditional departmental library of the **Computer Science Department, University of Peshawar**, into a modern digital platform.

The system tackles the everyday challenges faced by students and library staff — from inefficient manual record-keeping and difficulty in tracking issued books, to inconsistent fine calculation and limited access to digital resources — by providing a unified, intelligent, and user-friendly solution.

> **BiblioTrack bridges the gap between physical books and digital resources — all within a single integrated platform.**
